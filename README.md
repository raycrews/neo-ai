# Neo-AI

**A desktop writing workspace with optional AI assistance.**

Neo-AI helps authors organize story material, develop ideas, write manuscripts, and export their work. Keep chapters and scenes alongside character profiles, locations, worldbuilding, outlines, research, and notes.

Connect Neo-AI to local models through tools such as LM Studio or Ollama, or use a compatible online AI provider. Choose which documents the assistant can reference, keep separate conversations for different writing tasks, and save those conversations with your book.

## Features

- **Organize your book:** Arrange chapters, scenes, folders, and reference documents in one workspace.
- **Write and revise:** Use rich-text formatting, search, Find & Replace, spellcheck, and focus modes.
- **Develop ideas with AI:** Brainstorm, explore characters, refine outlines, and discuss your story using selected documents as context.
- **Revise selected passages:** Rewrite, expand, shorten, or give custom instructions. Review and edit the proposed text before accepting it.
- **Keep reusable instructions:** Store prompts tailored to particular models, genres, and writing tasks.
- **Protect your work:** Create backups and restore a separate copy of a library.
- **Export your writing:** Produce PDF, EPUB, DOCX, RTF, HTML, and plain-text files.
- **Personalize your workspace:** Choose themes, accent colors, and keyboard shortcuts, and open AI Assistance in a separate window.

Your writing stays in ordinary files in the library folder you choose. AI connections and device preferences are configured separately on each computer.

## Platforms

Available for **Windows** and **Linux**, with AppImage and Flatpak packages for Linux. An initial **Apple silicon Mac** test build is also available.

[Download Neo-AI](https://github.com/raycrews/neo-ai/releases)

## Origins

Neo-AI builds on **NEO by Hugh Howey**, extending the original writing application with integrated AI assistance, additional organizational tools, and desktop platform support.
